Your partner on your Home Screen

Keep using Fatties here. Install Scriptable on your iPhone to show your partner's last logged meal in a medium widget.

  1. Install Scriptable.
  2. Download the Fatties script, then share the file to Scriptable and add it. You can also copy its contents into a new script named Fatties.
  3. Create a connection below. Run Fatties in Scriptable and paste the connection code when prompted.
  4. Touch and hold your Home Screen, add a medium Scriptable widget, edit it, and choose the Fatties script.

iOS chooses refresh times. Tap the widget to open the meal's web page. Reconnect after 90 days, logging out, or changing your password.

Paste only into your Fatties script. This gives read-only access to your partner's latest meal and photo. If Copy doesn't work, touch and hold the code, choose Select All, then Copy.
