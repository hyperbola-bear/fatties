# Fatties

Shared meal tracking for two partners, each with their own nutrition plan.

## Language

**Partner**:
The other person in the pair using Fatties.

**Plan**:
A person's daily nutrition targets and weight goal, effective from its start date.

**Meal log**:
A saved record of food eaten by one person, including its food items, estimated nutrients, and eating time.

**Meal owner**:
The person whose food a meal log records. Each person creates and deletes only their own meal logs.

**Last logged meal**:
The most recently saved meal log, regardless of when the food was eaten.
_Avoid_: Latest meal, when the intended ordering could be ambiguous

**Food item**:
One component of a meal with its own portion and estimated nutrients.

**Portion multiplier**:
The amount of a food item eaten relative to the original estimate; ×1 means the estimated portion.

**Macro correction**:
A manual change to an individual nutrient estimate, separate from changing the amount of food eaten.

**Track**:
The shared view of both partners' meal history and progress, with Day, Week, Month, and Year time frames.

**Partner meal widget**:
A Home Screen view of the partner's last logged meal, including its photo, calories, and logging time.

**Logged time**:
The time a meal log was saved, distinct from the time the food was eaten.

**Eating time**:
The time the food was eaten, which may be earlier than the logged time.

**Refresh time**:
The last time the partner meal widget successfully fetched its current information.
